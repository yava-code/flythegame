## X / Twitter — main (EN)

I put the MaleCNS fruit-fly connectome (~166k neurons, frozen weights) in a fly swatter.

The fly never sees your hand.
It sees the shadow on the table.

η = dA/dt / A → LC4 + LPLC2 → DNp01 giant fiber → takeoff in ~50–150 ms.

Yellow freeze = the hit window.
Slap then. Wait and it’s gone.

Weights were never trained to play this.

## LinkedIn (EN)

Built a tiny tech demo around the MaleCNS fruit-fly connectome (~166k neurons).

Instead of scripting escape logic, the game feeds looming features from a table shadow into LC4 / LPLC2 and lets DNp01 decide takeoff — frozen weights, no training for the game.

There’s a real-vs-shuffled wiring toggle so you can feel what the connectome is actually buying you.
